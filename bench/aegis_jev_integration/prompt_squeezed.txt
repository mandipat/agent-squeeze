Server-side session and cart persistence layer. Backed by Redis with a Postgres write-through log for durability.

Use for anything involving shopper sessions, carts, or checkout state across devices.

| store_path | string | no | Snapshot directory, e.g. /var/lib/sessions |
## Session continuity
Session continuity: when a shopper signs in on a second device, the earlier session hands over its cart to the newer session. The handover preserves cart contents and the shopper sees a single merged cart.
